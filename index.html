What It Does

Everything in one place,
in plain language

No dedicated app. No complicated UI. Just message Liftwise the way you'd message a training partner.

Logging

Natural language workout logging

Send something like "bench press 3x5 @ 100kg RPE 8, shoulder a bit tight" — Liftwise parses it, structures it, and saves it to your sheet automatically.

History

Instant exercise history & coaching

Type any exercise name and get your last 10 sessions pulled up with coaching notes — trends, stalls, form patterns, and a key technique cue.

Q&A

Training questions with full context

Ask anything — "what else should I do today?", "should I deload?", "how's my squat trending?" — and get answers grounded in your actual data.

Sessions

Session tracking with auto-classification

Open and close sessions with a command. Liftwise logs duration, classifies the session type (push, pull, legs, etc.) and flags cardio or abs work.

Planning

AI-built training cycles

Tell Liftwise your goals, availability, and benchmarks. It builds a full week-by-week programme you can save and reference throughout your training.

Profile

Personalised to you

Save your height, weight, experience level, chronic injuries, and tendencies. Every answer accounts for who you are, not just what you logged today.

Corrections

Edit anything, anytime

Logged wrong? Just say "change my bench press to 95kg x 5" or edit the original message. Liftwise updates the sheet without duplicating data.

Injuries

Proactive injury awareness

Flag pain or tightness in your log — it's stored, tracked, and surfaced when relevant so advice always accounts for what's going on with your body.
